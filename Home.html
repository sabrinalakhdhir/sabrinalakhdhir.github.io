About Me

As a senior PhD candidate at the University of Victoria, I'm interested in exploring the concepts of making, digital fabrication, wearables, and customization through a social lens.

As a researcher, I am interested in understanding how people can alter, recreate, or redesign their current devices and belongings to better suit their personal needs and better express themselves. In my future work, I plan to explore these ideas through a social lens -- what makes a device socially acceptable or unacceptable? What interactions are individuals comfortable engaging in around what audiences?  How does environment impact what is considered acceptable? What constitutes a positive social experience? 

Personal devices are innately telling of their owners, and therefore, there is great value in understanding how the alteration of such devices for increased self-expression can change the ways in which people interact with or use such devices, in various environments and around different audiences.

Through my work, I aim to intersect the disciplines of art and technology such that individuals are empowered to be creative while also designing and developing meaningful artifacts for personal use.
